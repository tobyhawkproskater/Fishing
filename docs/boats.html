Intruder 22 (Hardtop)
New ~$90k+ · best used value
Lightest hull → best sand-flat draft and beaching. Tradeoffs: thinnest sides (.125"), narrower 8'3.5" beam, and a 225 HP cap. Offered as DuraFrame Sport Top or hardtop — matches your open-hardtop want.

Coho 21' Hard Top
$79,995 new (turnkey)
Your style favorite and an attainable NEW number. Open-bow hardtop; comes standard with 150 HP + trailer + hydraulic steering + offshore bracket. Cockpit is smaller (66") than the bigger hulls.

Seahawk 22' (Hardtop)
