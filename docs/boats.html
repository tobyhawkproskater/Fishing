Alumaweld
Intruder 22 (Hardtop)
New ~$90k+ · best used value
Lightest hull → best sand-flat draft and beaching. Tradeoffs: thinnest sides (.125"), narrower 8'3.5" beam, and a 225 HP cap. Offered as DuraFrame Sport Top or hardtop — matches your open-hardtop want.
Data: 2026 catalog PDF
North River
Coho 21' Hard Top
$79,995 new (turnkey)
The only true ~21' factory hardtop, and an attainable NEW number. Note it's North River's VALUE tier: .190" bottom + 7-yr warranty (vs the Seahawk's .250" + lifetime). Comes standard with 150 HP + trailer + hydraulic steering + offshore bracket. Cockpit is smaller (66") than the bigger hulls.
Data: dealer PDF
Top build
North River
Seahawk Outboard 21'
Used ~$60–98k
Best-built hull here: .250" bottom + towering 35" sides + North River's LIFETIME hull warranty (vs the Coho's .190" / 7-yr — same brand, value tier). 42° entry / 18° transom = soft, dry ride; verified offshore bracket. Ships with a deluxe FOLDING (soft) top standard — add the rigid removable top for a hardtop that pops off for sled-like deck space. The only catch: a true enclosed hardtop in this line means stepping up to the 23' Hard Top (25'2" LOA, 3,060 lb). Premium price — buy older/plainer to stay in budget.
Data: 2025 Seahawk spec sheet
KingFisher
2325 Coastal Express
~$120k+ new
Value-quality middle ground, now verified. Flatter 16° variable-deadrise hull = slightly firmer offshore ride but very stable. Note it's TRANSOM-mounted (no bracket) — 25" transom, step-thru — unlike the bracketed Duckworth/Alumaweld. Biggest tank here (85 gal) and a deep 6'2" cockpit; pilot-house cabin (7'2", 6'1" headroom). Capped at 250 HP.
Data: 2026 KingFisher boat guide PDF
Top value
Duckworth
Pacific Navigator 215
$55k (2015, live)
Verified-spec correction: at 2,270 lb dry it's the LIGHTEST hull here — not the deep-draft pig I'd assumed — with a full reverse-chine bottom, wide 8'6" beam, and an aggressive 34° bow entry for a soft open-water ride. Standard 25" offshore bracket. The live 2015 unit nails your dream rig: Yamaha 115 + T9.9 kicker, ~319 hrs, offshore-proven out of Westport. (Confirm the exact model — its 42-gal tank vs the 215's 65-gal standard hints it may be a Navigator 200/Sport.)
Data: 2015 Duckworth model guide PDF
