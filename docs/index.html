Scoring: wind_score × precip_score × (0.4 + 0.6 × tide_score). Wind and precip are hard multipliers; tide is a soft modifier so a glass-calm mid-cycle hour earns ~0.4 (Marginal) on big-swing days instead of being crushed to zero or inflated to Good. tide_score is 1.0 at slack and decays linearly to 0; the half-window is sized per side by the adjacent swing — a 13 ft drop into slack gives a tight ±45 min before-window, a 4 ft rise out gives a generous ±3 hr after-window. Thresholds: <3 ft = 6 hr, 3–6 = 3 hr, 6–9 = 90 min, ≥9 ft = 45 min. wind_score = 1.0/0.7/0.3/0.0 for gusts <12 / <18 / <25 / ≥25 mph. GREEN tide-curve overlay = slack window with calm wind. PRIME marker = slack with gust ≤10 & wind ≤7. Per-day badge: GLASS (max wind ≤7 & gust ≤10), BLOWN (max wind >15 or gust ≥25), BREEZY in between. Tide curve color matches the heatmap tier — green=Prime, light green=Good, yellow=Marginal, orange=Poor, red=Terrible. Daylight only (5 AM – 9 PM PT).
