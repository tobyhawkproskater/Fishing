Scoring (Chinook profile): wind_score × precip_score × (0.4 + 0.6 × tide_score). Wind and precip are hard multipliers; tide is a soft modifier so a glass-calm mid-cycle hour earns ~0.4 (Marginal/Good) on big-swing days instead of being crushed to zero or inflated to Good. tide_score is 1.0 at slack and decays linearly to 0; the half-window is sized per side by the adjacent swing — a 13 ft drop into slack gives a tight ±45 min before-window, a 4 ft rise out gives a generous ±3 hr after-window. Thresholds: <3 ft = 6 hr, 3–6 = 3 hr, 6–9 = 90 min, ≥9 ft = 45 min. wind_score takes the worse of two tiers — sustained <10 / <15 / <25 / ≥25 mph and gust <15 / <20 / <25 / ≥30 mph — mapped to 1.0/0.7/0.3/0.0 so a calm-but-gusty hour can't earn Prime. Slack-tide chart badge label always matches the heatmap tier (PRIME if cell ≥0.9, else GOOD) so the chart and the cell can't disagree. Gold badge + GLASS pill = slack with gust ≤10 & wind ≤7 AND the cell scores Prime — the standout combo. Per-day badge: GLASS (max wind ≤7 & gust ≤10), WINDY (max wind >15 or gust ≥25), BREEZY in between. Tide curve color matches the heatmap tier — green=Prime, light green=Good, yellow=Marginal, orange=Poor, red=Terrible. Daylight-gated: cell scores fall to 0 past civil dusk and linearly taper through morning/evening twilight so windows can't land in the dark. Wind blend: HRRR 80% + ECMWF 20% weighted blend (primary 0-24 h) + ECMWF IFS 0.25° (primary 24-168 h) + Open-Meteo (gfs_seamless+icon_seamless) (fallback) + NWS gridpoints (fallback). Other sources: NOAA NWS · NOAA CO-OPS · NDBC.
