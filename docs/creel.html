Rates are fish divided by interviewed anglers, aggregated by catch area, not ramp. HOT NOW marks at least 0.35 coho per angler on a 20-angler sample before enough history exists. SURGE compares the latest 3 sampled days with the prior 7 and requires at least 20 recent and 30 baseline anglers. Small samples are labeled LOW SAMPLE. WDFW calls these raw data subject to QA/QC; catch rate is not total run size or a forecast.
